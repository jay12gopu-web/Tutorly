Tutorly Help

How can we help?

Quick answers for getting started, studying with Tutorly, and managing your account.

Getting started

Sign in, finish student setup, then open Tutorly Chat. Use New Chat for a fresh topic and Search Chats to revisit earlier work.

Using Tutorly Chat

Ask naturally, include your class level when useful, and follow up with phrases like “simpler,” “show the next step,” or “give another example.”

Homework images

Use the attachment button to add a readable JPG, PNG, or WebP question. Check the preview before sending and avoid uploading private information.

Voice Chat

Dictation puts editable speech into the composer. Full Voice Chat listens and replies conversationally; close it anytime to release the microphone.

Plans and credits

Normal tutoring chat costs 0 credits. Premium tools show their credit cost before use. View your balance on Plan & Billing.

Account help

Open your bottom-left account menu for Profile, Settings, Personalization, Help, and secure sign out.

Still stuck?

Contact Tutorly Support with the page name and what you were trying to do. For a product issue, choose “Bug Report.”
