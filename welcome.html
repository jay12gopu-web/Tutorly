Maths: solve x + 5 = 10

Solving x + 5 = 10

To solve an equation, keep the balance the same on both sides.

Subtract 5 from both sides: x + 5 - 5 = 10 - 5.

Final answer: x = 5.

Physics: why do objects fall down?

Gravity

Objects fall because Earth pulls them with gravity.

This pull acts toward the center of Earth, so dropped objects move downward unless another force stops them.

Chemistry: what is a chemical reaction?

Chemical Reactions

A chemical reaction happens when substances change into new substances.

Reactants are the starting materials. Products are the new materials formed after bonds break and rearrange.

Biology: how does photosynthesis work?

Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process plants use to make food using sunlight. The plant takes in carbon dioxide from the air and water from the soil.

Inside the leaves, chlorophyll captures light energy. That energy helps turn carbon dioxide and water into glucose, which is the plant's food.

The main result is glucose for energy and oxygen released back into the air.

History: why was the Indus Valley important?

Indus Valley Civilization

The Indus Valley was one of the world's earliest urban civilizations.

It is important because cities like Harappa and Mohenjo-daro had planned streets, drainage systems, trade, and organized town life.

Geography: why do monsoons happen?

Monsoons

Monsoons happen because land and sea heat up at different speeds.

In summer, warm land pulls moist air from the ocean. That moist air rises, cools, and brings heavy rainfall.
