  3. Roots absorb water from soil.
  4. The plant makes glucose for food and releases oxygen.
  
  ### 5. Final Answer
  **Final answer:** Photosynthesis is the process by which plants make glucose and oxygen using sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water.
