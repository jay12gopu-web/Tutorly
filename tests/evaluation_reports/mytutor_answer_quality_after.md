  2. Faster particles collide more often.
  3. More particles have enough energy to overcome activation energy.
  4. So, successful collisions increase and the reaction rate rises.
  
  ### 5. Final Answer
  **Final answer:** Increasing temperature generally increases reaction rate because particles collide more often and with more energy.
  
  ### 6. Why This Works
  Reaction rate depends on successful collisions, not just any collision.
