Tutorly Privacy Policy

Effective 24 August 2026. This page explains the information Tutorly uses to provide the service.

Information we process

Tutorly may process account details, student setup preferences, chat content, uploaded homework images, voice transcripts, learning activity, subscription status, and technical service logs when you use those features.

Why we use it

We use this information to deliver tutoring, preserve chat history, personalize explanations, operate account and billing features, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

Voice and images

Microphone access occurs only when you start dictation or Voice Chat. Tutorly stops active microphone tracks when voice mode closes. Upload only homework content you are allowed to share.

Payments

Payments use Tutorly's server-side Razorpay flow. Tutorly does not place payment secrets in frontend code. Payment records are retained as needed for billing and support.

Your choices

You can change profile preferences, clear supported local learning data, sign out, or contact Tutorly to request lawful access, correction, or deletion.

Contact

For a privacy request, use the Tutorly contact page and select Account Help. Do not include passwords or payment credentials.
